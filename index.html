Play Store & Privacy

Privacy Policy & Data Safety

1. Zero Cloud Upload of Local Photos: All local camera roll or Windows folder triage, Canvas sharpness scoring, and swipe decisions happen locally on your device. SnapCull never uploads your local photos to third-party servers.

2. Google Photos Picker API: When you sign in with Google, SnapCull uses OAuth 2.0 (photospicker.mediaitems.readonly) strictly to let you pick photos in Google's official Picker UI and triage them in your session.

3. Safe Cull Bin Review: Swiping left or pressing ← moves photos into the Cull Bin first. Nothing is permanently removed until you tap Delete Forever.
